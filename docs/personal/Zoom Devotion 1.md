Proverbs 18:24 (NIV)  
“One who has unreliable friends soon comes to ruin, but there is a friend who sticks closer than a brother.”  
  
Generally, we know that no man is truly self-sufficient. We are all connected and dependent on one another.  
  
Biology teaches us interdependence in an ecosystem—how no species can survive in complete isolation.  
  
Symbiosis, a Greek word meaning “living together or with,” is another biological concept that further explains how certain individuals depend on one another.  
  
By mutualism, both species benefit from each other; by commensalism, only one benefits while the other is unaffected; and by parasitism, only one benefits while the other is affected.
